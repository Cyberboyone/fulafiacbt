# Privacy Policy for Fulafia CBT

**Effective Date:** August 13, 2026

## Introduction

Fulafia CBT ("we", "our", or "us") is a computer-based test practice application for 100-level courses at the Federal University of Lafia (FULafia). This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Advertising IDs**: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers, general location (country/region level), and interaction data with ads.
- **App Usage Analytics**: We use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage patterns (e.g., which features are used, crash reports). This data is aggregated and anonymized.

### Information You Provide
- **Profile Data**: You may create an in-app profile with a display name. This is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
- **Progress & Scores**: All quiz progress, scores, coins, and streaks are stored locally on your device.

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect sensitive personal information (payment data, government IDs, health data, precise location).
- We do not require account registration or login.
- We do not sell your data to third parties.

## Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services:
- **Google AdMob** (advertising): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Firebase Analytics** (usage analytics): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

These providers have their own privacy policies governing the use of data collected through their SDKs.

## Data Storage & Security

All personal learning data (scores, progress, profile) is stored locally on your device using secure local storage (Hive). We do not operate user-account servers and do not transmit this data externally.

## Children's Privacy

The app is intended for university students (ages 13+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Rights

Since most data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or using the in-app reset option.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page.
